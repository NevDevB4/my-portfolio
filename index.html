About Me

I’m Travis Baker — a developer and QA auditor passionate about building intelligent systems and clean web experiences. Before transitioning into tech, I founded and operated a bootstrapped construction startup as a sole proprietor, completing hundreds of residential and commercial projects across Fayetteville and surrounding areas. That experience taught me how to manage operations, budgets, and client relationships while solving complex problems under pressure.

Today, I’m pursuing my Associate’s Degree in Software Engineering, combining my practical, hands‑on mindset with technical precision. My background in construction gives me a unique perspective on structure, efficiency, and real‑world problem solving — qualities I now apply to software development and AI systems.

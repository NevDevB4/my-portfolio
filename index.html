About Me

I’m Travis Baker — a developer, QA auditor, and AI data specialist passionate about building intelligent systems and clean web experiences. Before transitioning into tech, I founded and operated a bootstrapped construction startup as a sole proprietor, completing hundreds of residential and commercial projects across Fayetteville and surrounding areas. I personally drafted and managed all service contracts for Thumbs Up Commercial and Residential Services, gaining hands‑on experience in business operations, client negotiation, and project management.

My frontend development experience can be observed firsthand through this portfolio. I’ve worked extensively with JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, and HTML to create responsive interfaces and interactive systems. I’ve successfully implemented virtual machines such as Kali Linux and Ubuntu, flashed open‑source operating systems onto otherwise unusable machines, and repurposed them for development and experimentation. I also built and maintained a home mining machine for Monero, contributing to the cryptocurrency economy as a miner and maintaining my own node — gaining firsthand experience in decentralized systems and network reliability.

In the AI space, I’ve worked as a data annotator with Outlier, contributing to the Aether Project — a large‑scale initiative focused on improving AI reasoning and data quality. As a QA Auditor at Alignerr, I review and refine AI outputs to ensure precision, consistency, and ethical data handling. These roles have strengthened my understanding of how structured data and human insight combine to train reliable, high‑performing models.

I’m also the creator behind Disorderly & Entertaining, a growing content brand that explores storytelling and audience engagement. My creative background complements my technical work, allowing me to approach development with both analytical and narrative depth.

Academically, I’m pursuing my Associate’s Degree in Software Engineering, having completed PY103 and FE102 coursework. My studies, combined with self‑driven experimentation in Linux systems, Devvit Web development, and AI annotation, have given me a well‑rounded foundation in both software and data engineering.

Across all my work — from construction and entrepreneurship to AI and software — I focus on structure, efficiency, and real‑world problem solving. I build systems that work, whether they’re physical, digital, or intelligent.
